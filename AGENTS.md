## Mandatory project onboarding for every AI

Before planning implementation, claiming a task, or editing files, read these files **in full and in order**:

1. [agent.md](./agent.md) — mandatory rules, role ownership, claims and handoffs.
2. [roadmap.md](./roadmap.md) — current product scope, stages, task IDs and acceptance criteria.
3. [process.md](./process.md) — current progress, active owners, decisions, blockers and evidence.

Then read the relevant handoff prompt/contract and verify the actual branch, HEAD and working tree. Record your role/session, task ID and file scope in process.md before the first edit. Update progress and append an evidence-backed handoff before ending. Do not overwrite another AI's work or claim an occupied task. Re-read current process state when resuming or changing machines. These rules apply to Codex, Claude and the PC agent; see agent.md for cross-machine reconciliation.
